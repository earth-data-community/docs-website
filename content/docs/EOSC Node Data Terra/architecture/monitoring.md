# Service monitoring

>[!Note] 
>Information about the quality and availability of services and resources made available through EOSC Nodes into the EOSC Federation.

For Monitoring, a similar approach to Helpdesk will be adopted by relying on a dedicated instance provided through EOSC Mesh. Data Terra services, which already expose health-check endpoints, will be monitored from this centralized instance, providing a unified view of service availability and operational status across the node.
