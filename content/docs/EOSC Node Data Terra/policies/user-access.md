# Acceptable Use Policy – EOSC Node Data Terra
Version updated, 5 October 2026

## **Introduction**

This Acceptable Use Policy and Conditions of Use (“AUP”) defines the rules and conditions that govern your access to and use, including transmission, processing and storage of data, of the resources and services provided through the **EOSC Node** **Data Terra**.   
The EOSC Node Data Terra provides access to resources, including data and other digital resources made available within the framework of the European Open Science Cloud (EOSC), in support of research and innovation related to the Earth system, biodiversity and the environment.  
The EOSC Node Data Terra provides access to digital services made available within the EOSC framework. Access to the EOSC Node Data Terra does not necessarily imply access to all services made available through it, and access to individual services may be subject to additional terms and conditions established by the relevant service provider.

## **Requirements**

1. You shall only use the Services in accordance with their stated purposes, applicable policies and conditions of use, and shall show consideration towards other users.  
2. You shall only use the Services for lawful purposes and shall not breach, attempt to breach, or circumvent administrative, access-control or security mechanisms.  
3. You shall respect intellectual property rights, licences, confidentiality requirements and any other applicable restrictions concerning the resources accessed through the Services.  
4. You shall ensure that any resources you transmit, process or store through the Services may lawfully be used for the intended purpose.  
5. You shall protect your access credentials, including passwords, private keys, access tokens and multi-factor authentication mechanisms. Intentional sharing of credentials is not permitted.  
6. You shall keep your registration and account information accurate and up to date.  
7. You shall use the Services responsibly and shall not intentionally interfere with their availability, performance, security or proper operation, or unreasonably affect other users.  
8. You shall promptly report known or suspected security incidents, compromised credentials or misuse of the Services to the security contact specified below.  
9. You shall comply with any additional terms, conditions, licences, quotas or usage restrictions applicable to a specific Service.  
10. Your personal data will be processed in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.  
11. Your access to the Services may be restricted or suspended for administrative, operational, legal or security reasons, including in the event of a violation of this AUP.  
12. If you violate this AUP, appropriate measures may be taken, including suspension or termination of your access and, where appropriate, notification of your home organisation, the relevant Service Provider or competent authorities.

## **Revisions to this Acceptable Use Policy**

The EOSC Node Data Terra reserves the right to revise, amend or modify this AUP when necessary. The current version of the AUP will be made available through the Data Terra EOSC Node landing page.  
Continued use of the Services after the effective date of a revised AUP constitutes acceptance of the revised version.

# User Access Policy – EOSC Node Data Terra
This User Access Policy (“UAP”) explains who may use the resources and services of the Data Terra Node, and how you obtain access to them.

## **Federated authentication**

- Users may access the service through supported federated identity providers, such as:  
  - eduGAIN / institutional identity providers;  
  - ORCID;  
  - EOSC-compatible AAI services  
- Some services are open and they can be used anonymously or immediately after signing in. Other services need an access request. The exact attributes received depend on the identity provider and on the requirements of the service being accessed through the Node services catalogue.  
- When authentication is performed through an institutional identity provider, the service may receive the user's name and email address and, where required, institutional affiliation or other authorised attributes.  
- If a required attribute is not provided by the user's Home Organisation, the service may ask the user to provide it directly.  
- When ORCID is used as an authentication or identification provider, only the attributes required by the relevant service are retrieved from ORCID.  
- The access may be restricted, suspended or withdrawn if the authorisation requirements are not longer met, for administrative, operational, or security reasons, without prior notice and without compensation, or if not compliant with the Acceptable Use Policy or the applicable policies and notices from Data Terra.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data are processed when users access the services of the EOSC Node Data Terra**,** including its federated authentication and authorisation services.

## **2\. Personal data processed**

Depending on the authentication method and the service accessed, the EOSC Node Data Terra may process the following categories of personal data:

* first name and surname;  
* email address;  
* institutional affiliation / organisation;  
* country;  
* professional or organisational information required for authorisation;  
* persistent or pseudonymous identifiers associated with the user's federated account;  
* information concerning the authentication method used;  
* account and authorisation information;  
* technical information and security logs associated with access to the service.

The EOSC Node Data Terra only processes attributes that are necessary for the relevant service and its stated purposes.

## **3\. Purposes of processing**

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

* authenticating users;  
* creating and managing user accounts;  
* identifying users within the EOSC Node;  
* determining access rights and permissions;  
* providing access to authorised services and resources;  
* enabling federated access across EOSC services;  
* ensuring the security and integrity of the services;  
* detecting and investigating security incidents or misuse;  
* diagnosing and resolving technical problems;  
* maintaining and improving the reliability and security of the infrastructure;  
* fulfilling applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Personal data will not be processed for purposes incompatible with those described above.

## **4\. Legal basis**

Depending on the processing activity and the applicable organisational framework, personal data may be processed on one or more of the following legal bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

* performance of a contract or provision of a requested service;  
* compliance with a legal obligation;  
* legitimate interests, in particular the security, maintenance and proper operation of the infrastructure and services, where these interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the data subject;  
* consent, where consent is required and is the applicable legal basis.

The applicable legal basis may vary depending on the service and the category of processing concerned.

## **5\. Disclosure of personal data**

Personal data may be disclosed to or made available to:

* the Data Terra services and applications that the user has requested to access;  
* authorised service providers acting on behalf of Data Terra, where applicable;  
* federated identity providers and authentication infrastructures involved in the authentication process;  
* other EOSC services or infrastructures, where this is necessary to provide the requested service and is covered by the applicable access and data protection arrangements;  
* competent public authorities where disclosure is required by law.

User attributes are transmitted to a service only when necessary for authentication, identification or authorisation.  
Data Terra does not sell personal data or use them for commercial advertising purposes.

## **6\. International transfers**

The EOSC Node Data Terra operates within a European and international research infrastructure environment. Where personal data are transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), such transfers will be carried out in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR and appropriate safeguards will be implemented where required, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards.  
Information concerning a specific international transfer and the safeguards applied may be obtained by contacting the Data Terra privacy contact.

## **7\. Data retention**

Personal data are retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which they were collected, taking into account applicable legal, security and operational requirements.  
The deletion of an account does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all technical or security logs where their retention remains necessary for security, legal or audit purposes.

## **8\. Security**

Data Terra implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.  
The EOSC Node Data Terra applies privacy and security principles by design and by default.

## **9\. Your rights**

You may access or rectify your personal data, or request deactivation of your account, by contacting the Helpdesk. If you have any questions regarding your personal data or your data protection rights, please contact the Helpdesk.  
Please note that some personal data, such as attributes provided by your Home Organisation (e.g. your university or research institute), may be managed by that organisation. To access or rectify such data, please contact your Home Organisation’s IT helpdesk.  
Where applicable, you may object to the processing of your personal data by contacting the Helpdesk.

## **10\. Cookies and similar technologies**

The EOSC Node Data Terra may use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary for authentication, session management, security and operation of the service.  
Where cookies or similar technologies are used for additional purposes requiring consent under applicable law, the relevant consent mechanism and information will be provided separately.

## **11\. Changes to this Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the services, technical infrastructure, applicable legislation or EOSC Federation requirements.  
The latest version will be made available on the EOSC Node Data Terra website or on the relevant service interface.

## **12\. Applicable data protection framework**

The EOSC Node Data Terra is committed to applying the principles of the GDPR.  The EOSC Node also takes into account the applicable EOSC Federation requirements and good practices concerning data protection, security and privacy.
